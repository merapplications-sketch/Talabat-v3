Expected EN persists; source predicts RU. **No screenshot/browser execution in this report.**

Owner: programmer for a separately approved isolated demo-only correction; independent UX/QA for validation at RU/EN and reload. Acceptance: EN remains EN across reload, RU remains RU and first-visit preference follows approved browser-language policy; no changes to root approved source/design. **No fix applied or commissioned.**
